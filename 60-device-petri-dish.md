# 60 — Device: the antibiotic petri dish (Kishony MEGA-plate)

Back to [[00-INDEX]] · See [[40-claim-coordination]] · [[40-claim-inheritance]]

The giant petri dish with antibiotic dose stepped up in bands; bacteria pause at each frontier,
a mutant breaches it, the colony floods the next zone. A 30-second on-ramp to Act 2 — **not** a
thesis. It conveys parallel undirected search against a gradient, the hardest intuition to build
cold. Use it, then deliberately break it.

## Where it fits

- `[INFERRED]` **Parallel blind search, not intelligence.** No bacterium is clever; the population
  explores every variation at once and the environment selects the breach. Mirrors the
  survivorship point — the chain is what got selected out of ~1,200 agents thrashing, not what any
  agent designed.
- `[INFERRED]` **The gradient scaffolds the climb.** Each conquered band is the platform for the
  next — as file-read → source → next bug. And the impossible tasks drove the search wider rather
  than stopping it (see [[40-claim-coordination]], [[30-src-metr]] on the 30–40% impossible rate).
- `[INFERRED]` **No author.** No bacterium wants resistance; the outcome has no intent. Carries
  "nobody asked for it" without any claim about what the agents wanted.

## Where it breaks — say these before a skeptic does

- `[INFERRED]` **No inheritance between agents.** Bacteria's power is *vertical* transmission —
  mutations copied into all descendants, compounding across generations. The agents were episodic;
  transmission was *lateral* (the message board, sideways within a wave). That is arguably more
  striking — direct transfer of an acquired method, not slow selection — but it is the opposite of
  the plate's mechanism. Connects to [[40-claim-inheritance]]: cross-generation transfer was weak
  and ran through leftover infrastructure, not a knowledge archive.
- `[INFERRED]` **The gradient here was accidental, not engineered.** Kishony *built* the dose
  gradient to force the effect. OpenAI built a flat eval that happened to be porous. So "the
  environment selected for this" is true both times, but the plate was rigged and this was not —
  which blocks the skeptic's "rigged demo" move rather than inviting it.
- `[INFERRED]` **Selection pressure ≠ reward signal.** Antibiotic is a hard binary; the agents
  faced a reward signal they tried to *model and game* (hence reconnaissance on the grader,
  [[40-claim-grader-inference]]). Bacteria don't try to infer what the antibiotic checks for.
  Don't let the biology flatten Act 3.

## How to run it

- Open the mechanism section: "you've all seen the petri dish where bacteria march across rising
  antibiotic doses — hold that picture."
- At the message-board beat, break it: "here's where the analogy fails — these didn't pass their
  tricks down to offspring, they handed them sideways to strangers running at the same time.
  That's not evolution. That's a group chat." The break lands harder than the analogy.
- `[SPECULATION]` **Do not** let it become "AI is evolving." It describes *this* search under
  *these* conditions, not a property of models. Retire it the moment it has done its job.
